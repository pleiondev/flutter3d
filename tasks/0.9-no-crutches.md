# No crutches, special cases or fitted values — plan (2026-10-07)

An audit of everything the water-and-fire work added (core, effects package,
nine games) found the same few gaps in the core and the effects package
behind most of the games' workarounds. They go first; each game's crutches
come out once the law they stood in for is there.

## Core — heat and fire (`f3d_heat.c`)

- [x] A flame column gives no more than its fire releases: its width from
      the burning patch (as its length already is), its convective share
      (1 − χr)·Q shared among what stands in it by the plume each
      intercepts, the gas at the plume's centreline excess (Heskestad /
      McCaffrey ΔT(z, Q)) past the continuous flame.
- [x] The burning patch keeps its own heat balance, and goes out when it
      cannot hold itself alight (critical heat flux), not only by water.
- [x] Flame spread V = Φ / (kρc·(T_ig − T_s)²), Φ a material parameter;
      upward spread along −g apart from lateral; no speed cap — a skin at
      T_ig is involved where it is above it; the first patch from what lit it.
- [x] Contact ignition takes a time to ignite (semi-infinite solid under the
      contact flux), not one frame.
- [x] Convection by the body's size: forced (Whitaker / Churchill–Bernstein)
      with natural (Churchill–Chu); the wind-chill formula and the test that
      repeats its constant go.
- [x] Contact area from Hertz with the contact's load and a modulus in the
      material, not from the solver's leftover overlap; flat faces by area.
- [x] A part's flame front crosses into the next part of a compound at their
      shared face, so a beam burns the same however it was cut.
- [x] A burner: a body fed fuel at a rate (torches, braziers, tended fires).
- [x] An explosion: energy in, an impulse falling off with distance on the
      area facing it, a heat pulse.
- [x] Heat sees the water: a body's wetted share cooled by the liquid,
      quenched, boiling.
- [x] Flame feedback and burn rate not given twice: ṁ'' = q''_net / L_v.

## Core — water (`f3d_shallow.c`)

- [x] A body in water by its shape: submerged volume and centroid per
      column from the shape clipped by the surface, so a box rights itself
      and a log is long; drag and added mass by shape.
- [x] Kinematic and fixed bodies displace water and make wakes.
- [x] An outlet: a stage–discharge sink (weir law) and open edges with a
      prescribed inflow or outflow, so pools keep their level and a current
      runs without drains and ramps.
- [x] Walls in the grid (a mask), so no hidden raised rims.
- [x] Roughness per cell; flow over a free overfall held to critical depth.
- [x] A fall decided by the flow, not by a drop larger than a cell.
- [x] A body with no wet ring still floats; buoyancy coupled implicitly, not
      measured at a predicted position; drag implicit, not clamped.
- [x] Splash share and crown from a measured law (2026-10-08). A body's
      crown by the face it meets the water with: flat, Peters, van der Meer
      and Gordillo (JFM 724, 2013) — out at 1.6 v, one in one, drops past
      We 140, their size the air cushion's cut-off length; rounded, Aristoff
      and Bush (JFM 619, 2009) — a curtain straight up at 0.25 v while its
      sheet Weber number passes one. Falling water splashing off a film:
      Marengo and Tropea (1999) for the speeds, Mundo and colleagues (1995)
      for the drops. The picked 0.3, 53° and 1 cm and their setter are gone.
- [x] Rest threshold a world setting, the wind over the whole grid.

## Core — vehicles and chains

- [x] The damper integrated implicitly (or a soft constraint), no m/dt clamp.
- [x] Rolling resistance that holds like static friction, so a parked car
      needs no brake.
- [x] A motor's speed apart from a position servo with its own gain.
- [x] Wheel width a wheel's parameter.

## Effects package

- [x] Fire's size from the burning body and its heat, no per-view
      `baseWidth`; one FireView for fires of any size.
- [x] Firelight per fire or per cluster, luminous output from the radiant
      share and a blackbody, colour from its temperature, exposure doing the
      rest; no curves or caps in the package or the games.
- [x] Wind per fire; plume width from height (0.12 z), off-axis speed from
      the real distance; drag times from size; smoke albedo cited and a
      parameter.
- [x] Mist from the power of what lands, opacity per length, a real option
      rather than a node found by name.
- [x] Water: one absorption/scattering pair shared with the seabed; ripples
      faded by their size on screen; slope variance from the wind
      (Cox–Munk); under-the-surface by side, not by height; flow without a
      ±4 m/s ceiling; drops and bubbles lit, not glowing.
- [x] Hearing's mapping set by the game.

## Engine

- [x] Shadows: the far cascade's bias and normal offset by its texel, the
      cascade fitted to the shadow distance; racing drops its front-faces
      setting.
      *Done.* The cause was a depth bias of 0.0015 of each cascade's depth
      range, which on the far cascade came to 2.24 m along the light.
      - The bias is now capped at the nearest cascade's metres.
      - The cascade's depth is fitted along the light to the casters' box.
      - A 1.2 m box 31 m away now draws 175 of 211 shadow pixels, against
        58 before.
      - Racing's dark ribbons were measured against ray-traced truth and
        are real shadows, so racing draws both faces again.
      - Goldens were re-recorded on Impeller (25), the CPU (22) and WebGL
        (25).
      - Open: the WebGPU set is not re-recorded; and the near cascades'
        0.18 m floor wants reversed or 32-bit depth (A2.8 of
        `0.10-plan.md`, now in 1.0).

## Games — once the above is in

- Racing: wheels as kinematic bodies of their own shape; wrecks lit by what
  happened, not set hot; several fires at once.
- Platformer: outlets instead of a level controller; real roughness; wood
  righted by buoyancy, no torque, no breath of air, no putting back; hazards
  dressed by the level, not by a name table.
- River Sortie: a bed that falls at each weir; one scrolling grid; the game's
  plane left alone.
- Dungeon: no app-side flank heat or blast numbers; no watering fires to cap
  them; torches as burners; one FireView.
- Strategy: hits and brands from sim events; no temperature resets to cap
  fires; river from map data, carved into the drawn ground.
- Hollow: bombs thrown from the crater; contact into straw by the core; the
  car rolls and rests by itself; roof as one body if the core spreads over
  a surface.
- Reef: the current from open edges, no push-back and no ramp; the boat
  comes to the find; finds with their displaced volume.
- Arcade, sandbox: litter and planks lit by the core, not set hot.
