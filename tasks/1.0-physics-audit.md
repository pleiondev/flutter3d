# Physics audit before rc.1 (2026-10-09)

Four read-only audits covered light, optics and materials; mechanics; heat, fire, sound and chemistry; and the world's properties. Their summaries are in the session scratchpad, `physics_audit/1..4`.

## Dmitrii's decisions

1. **One gravity per game.** The engine's default stays 9.81. Each game sets its own world's gravity explicitly (platformer 24, racing 20, the dungeon and shooter whatever they play at now), and everything in that game reads it: characters, rigid bodies, particles, ragdolls, AI jump arcs, tyre grip and buoyancy. A Moon level then works in every game.
2. **The physical sky is drawn correctly** (it was π too dark). The goldens are re-recorded.
3. **One set of reference values at 20 °C**, shared by C and Dart: water 998.2 kg/m³, 1.002 mPa·s, 0.0728 N/m, 4182 J/(kg·K); oil and mercury also at 20 °C.
4. **Ragdoll masses follow de Leva (1996)**, with one reference body of 73 kg (ICRP 89).

## Fixes without a decision (errors)

- **Sun's angular size.** `sunAngularRadius` should be 0.2666°, not the diameter. One constant is shared with the shadows.
- **Seabed.** The path through the water uses the refracted angle. The sun blur is σ = w/4.
- **Missed migration to lux.** `Daylight.defaultSunIntensity`, the project template, the showcase and the double conversion in glTF lights. A rule catches small literals passed to `intensity:`.
- **Missing division by π:** emissive in arcade (meteors) and strategy (embers).
- **Chemlab:**
  - HCl is colourless;
  - KMnO₄ is opaque at the stated concentration, or the label changes;
  - Beer–Lambert runs in linear light;
  - "colour mixing only, no reactions" is stated.
- **Pendulum lab:**
  - a fixed step;
  - ω is rescaled by (L/L′)² when the length changes;
  - damping units;
  - the period correction at large angles;
  - the showcase formula.
- **Swimming:** buoyancy depends on g (or on densities).
- **Ballistic `ParticleGravity(-N)`:** use the world's gravity.
- **Determinism:**
  - `NativeDynamics` no longer writes gravity into the core every step;
  - gravity and `airPressure` go into the snapshot;
  - a test changes gravity mid-run, then rewinds and replays.
- **World properties.**
  - One `WorldProperties` type, which the level format and plugin_api read.

  - The speed of sound follows the air temperature.
  - Wind reaches cloth and particles.
  - The forced vacuum in `NativeDynamics` is removed.
  - Racing `airDrag` follows the world's ρ.

## Physical materials catalog (Dmitrii, 2026-10-09)

- **What it covers.** Every material is in one catalog, not only fluids.
  `PhysicalMaterial` has a phase and groups of properties:
  - mechanical: density, elasticity, friction and restitution;
  - fluid: viscosity and surface tension;
  - thermal: heat capacity, conductivity, phase changes and combustion;
  - acoustic: absorption by band, speed of sound;
  - optical: refractive index, absorption, metal F0.

  Each group has a source. New groups can be added without breaking
  anything.
- **`MaterialCatalog` is a PluginRegistry.**
  - Built-in entries are `f3d.*`; a plugin's are `<pluginId>.<name>`.
  - A `materials:` section in `.f3dplugin`.
  - A conformance check.
  - Material pairs, with a contact override.
- **Who reads it.** Colliders, cloth, fluids, fire and sound occlusion.
  Explicit numbers stay as overrides. The C header is generated from the
  built-in entries.
- **Storage inside `.f3d`.** An optional `physicalMaterials` section that
  0.8 readers skip. A render material refers to a physical one by id or
  carries its own record. Levels and prefabs refer by id, and a level that
  names an unknown id is refused, naming the plugin it needs. This is
  done after the catalog's API is settled.

## Expanding the catalog (Dmitrii, 2026-10-09)

- **Coverage.** About 100 common fluids (liquids and gases) and about 100
  solids, in `flutter3d_matter`. The solids include rubbers and other
  materials that deform inelastically.
- **A new property group for inelastic deformation:**
  - hyperelasticity: neo-Hookean, Mooney–Rivlin, Ogden or Yeoh, with
    parameters;
  - viscoelasticity: a Prony series or tan δ, with Tg;
  - plasticity: yield, hardening (e.g. Johnson–Cook), elongation at break;
  - damping (loss factor), and rebound resilience.
- **Sources.** Every value has a source; an estimate is marked as one; a
  value with no source is not entered.
- **Order.**
  1. Two research agents gather the data into the scratchpad
     (`matter_data/fluids.json`, `solids.json`) while step A of the
     boundaries is running.
  2. After step A, the data goes into matter, along with the new group, its
     codec, the conformance check and the generated C header.
