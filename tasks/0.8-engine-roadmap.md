# flutter3d 0.8 — the engine roadmap

Date: 2026-09-23. Branch `hal-contract-0.8`, at `6170558f` ("The stack moves
to 0.8.0, unpublished"). Written from five parallel research passes over the
2024–2026 literature and engine releases, each told the constraints below and
each made to read the code before ranking anything, then narrowed by the
owner's answers to eighteen questions.

## 0. In short

1. **The goal of 0.8 is picture quality, measured on a phone.** Impeller on
   Android and iOS is the platform new work is judged on; WebGL2 stays the
   floor, with WebGPU-only extras allowed behind a capability flag.
2. **The software rasteriser mirrors every new effect, temporal ones
   included.** Jitter, noise and history are functions of the frame index, so
   a scripted sequence of frames is a golden like any other.
3. **The contract comes first.** Typed uniform blocks, a per-pass profiler and
   command traces land before the effects that would otherwise add dozens of
   new ways for a misspelled member to read zero.
4. **Everything below ships as 0.8.** That is thirty-odd items; §9 says what
   that costs and where the order bends if it has to.

## 1. The constraints every item was ranked against

- No compute on the main backend. flutter_gpu 3.47 has vertex and fragment
  stages only; compute is an umbrella issue (flutter/flutter#188474) with the
  pipeline and dispatch half still open (#188480).
- Shaders are compiled ahead of time; one render pass per command buffer.
- Readback is a frame or two late and RGBA8 only; a depth texture cannot be
  sampled, so depth lives in colour targets.
- MRT is probed, not assumed (`probeMultipleRenderTargets`), and the surface
  buffer turns MSAA off for the frame.
- Four backends must agree, one of which is pure Dart.

What the research ruled out for that reason, and does not come back until a
constraint moves: Nanite-style cluster DAGs, visibility buffers, ReSTIR and
MegaLights, FSR 2/3/4, linked-list or moment OIT, neural texture sampling,
cooperative vectors.

## 2. What the code already has that the plan builds on

Read, not assumed, by the research passes:

- **An irradiance field nobody samples per pixel.** `irradiance_gather.dart`
  bakes octahedral irradiance plus two Chebyshev moments with the CPU
  raycaster; `renderer_mesh_encode.dart:_applyIrradiance` reads it at a mesh's
  bounds centre in two directions and overwrites the hemisphere ambient.
- **Lights are chosen per object, not per pixel.** Eight through `FragInfo`,
  twenty-four more through the light-list texture, scored on the CPU. A large
  floor saturates at thirty-two.
- **PBR uses Karis's analytic EnvBRDF**, so multiscatter compensation is
  arithmetic on terms already computed.
- **`flutter3d_mesh/qem_simplify.dart`** is a Garland–Heckbert simplifier with
  seam penalties; automatic LOD is wiring, not an algorithm.
- **The splat sort is a comparator with a closure** (`splat_cloud.dart:120`)
  while `key_sort.dart` has a radix sort.
- **glTF material extensions stop at unlit and emissive_strength**; a file
  that *requires* clearcoat, sheen or transmission is refused.
- **`stage_bindings.dart`** is already generated from impellerc's reflection,
  and `testing_recorded.dart`'s sealed `Recorded` hierarchy is an IR for a
  command stream.
- **`vertex_texture_checks.dart`** already probes vertex texture fetch, which
  is what GPGPU-through-fragments needs.

## 3. Hardware contract (`flutter3d_hardware`) — first

**H1. Typed uniform blocks generated from reflection.** Extend
`flutter3d_impeller/tool/stage_bindings.dart` to emit member name, type,
std140 offset and array stride per block, and generate
`flutter3d_shaders/lib/uniform_blocks.dart`: one `final class` per block with
typed setters writing into a `Float32List`, and a `written` bitmask a test
holds against the members reflection says are read. A misspelled member
becomes a compile error; a forgotten one becomes a failing test. A generated
check compares the offsets with `UniformSlot.getMemberOffsetInBytes`
(Impeller), naga's WGSL layout and WebGL's `UNIFORM_OFFSET`, so three
toolchains that disagree say so. The CPU backend keeps its name map behind an
adapter. Prior art: sokol-shdc, wgsl_bindgen with encase, Filament's matc.

**H2. A profiler by pass.** `beginRenderPass(…, label:)`, CPU spans around
every pass sent to `dart:developer` `Timeline`, a `FrameTimings` record per
frame, `supportsGpuTimestamps` answered true on WebGPU (`timestampWrites`,
100 µs quantised) and by `EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2` where a browser has
it; Impeller answers false until flutter/flutter#190405 merges, then maps one
to one. Every item below states its cost on the Galaxy A55 through this.

**H3. `.f3dtrace`: a command stream as a file.** Serialise `Recorded` plus
the resource contents; `ReplayDevice.run(trace, device)` turns "wrong on the
phone" into something that replays on the VM and in a browser.

**H4. Differential and metamorphic fuzzing.** A seeded generator of valid
command streams — pipelines, blend and stencil, viewports and scissors,
instancing, render-to-mip — run on every backend and compared by readback,
plus transforms that must not change the picture: split a draw, reorder
disjoint draws, fold an identity into a uniform, add a copy hop. Failures
shrink by delta debugging over H3's streams. A second arm feeds generated
GLSL through impellerc and naga and compares. Prior art: GraphicsFuzz
(OOPSLA 2017), WebGlitch (ECOOP 2025), WGSLsmith.

**H5. `FieldPass`: GPGPU through fragment passes.** Ping-pong float targets
plus vertex texture fetch, above the HAL and on all four backends. It is what
GPU particles, probe updates (L4) and reduction chains stand on. Three new
conformance checks: float render targets per format, float VTF, NaN and
denormal agreement.

**H6. An optional compute capability.** Shaped after Flutter's own design so
Impeller becomes a mapping when #188480 lands: `supportsCompute`,
`createStorageBuffer`, `createComputePipeline`, `beginComputePass`,
`dispatch(x, y, z)`, `readBuffer`. WebGPU and the CPU backend implement it in
0.8; Impeller and WebGL2 answer false. Every caller keeps an H5 or CPU path.
`readBuffer` closes "no buffer readback" on three backends at once.

## 4. The frame: temporal accumulation

**R1. Jitter and motion vectors.** Halton(2,3) by `frameIndex mod 16`; an
`rg16f` velocity target with jitter removed. Camera motion is reconstructed
in a full-screen pass from depth and `prevViewProj · inv(curViewProj)`, with
no MRT needed; nodes that move themselves get a velocity-only pass with a
depth-equal test, fed from the previous model matrix, joint palette, morph
weights or instance buffer, all double-buffered. The sky and particles are
masked. Golden: a velocity picture of a rigid, a skinned and a morphed mesh.
Depends on H1.

**R2. TAAU, written as an upscaler from the start.** A nearest-depth 3×3
velocity, Catmull-Rom history, variance clipping in YCoCg, luminance
weighting, disocclusion and camera-cut rejection, a negative mip bias of
`log2(renderScale) − 0.5`, history kept pre-exposed so auto exposure does not
smear it. Input size and output size differ, so `renderScale` and
`AdaptiveScale` get quality back rather than losing it. Modelled on Snapdragon
GSR2's two-pass fragment variant (BSD-3). RCAS runs after it to put back the
sharpness history takes. Goldens: sixteen static frames converging towards a
supersampled CPU reference, a thin-railing scene in motion, both at scale 1.0
and 0.6. Depends on R1.

**R3. STBN and temporal denoising.** NVIDIA's spatiotemporal blue-noise
slices shipped as ordinary textures, indexed by frame; SSAO, SSR and contact
shadows drop to a few samples a pixel and reproject their own history.
Depends on R2.

## 5. Light

**L1. Multiscatter GGX.** Fdez-Agüera's compensation on the split-sum terms
already computed, Turquin's scale for punctual lights. A few lines per model.

**L2. AgX and ACES 2.0.** Both baked offline into the existing 3D LUT, the
analytic transforms living only in the Dart that writes the LUT, so the
picture is the same bytes on every backend.

**L3. The irradiance field, per pixel.** Upload the field as an octahedral
atlas (irradiance `rgba16f`, moments) and sample the eight surrounding probes
with trilinear × Chebyshev × backface weights and a normal and view bias, as
DDGI does. `_applyIrradiance` goes.

**L4. Probes updated on the GPU.** A low-resolution cube per probe drawn with
the normal shading path, convolved into its octahedral tile with hysteresis
by a fragment pass, one to four probes a frame round-robin; multiple bounces
come from the capture reading the field. The CPU raycaster stays as the
reference. Depends on L3, H5.

**L5. GTAO, then screen-space indirect light by visibility bitmask**
(Therrien et al. 2023): a 32-sector bitfield in place of two horizon angles,
the same loop reading last frame's lit colour for indirect diffuse. Replaces
the 12-tap SSAO. Depends on R3 for its noise.

**L6. Clustered forward, clusters built on the CPU.** A 16×9×24 froxel grid
with logarithmic depth, filled in Dart, uploaded as a header texture and an
index texture; the light texture stays as the light data. Ends the 8+24 cap
and the per-draw index upload. Depends on H1.

**L7. LTC for rectangle lights.** The two 64×64 tables shipped as an asset
and sampled by the software rasteriser from the same bytes.

## 6. Shadows and air

**S1. Cached and scrolling cascades.** The static and dynamic split the cube
atlas already has, applied to the far cascades; a texel-snapped shift becomes
a blit of the old tile plus a scissored draw of the strip that appeared.

**S2. EVSM.** Depth is already in a colour target; writing `exp(±c·d)` costs
nothing more, and blurring and mipping give wide soft shadows. Needs
`rgba32f` and linear float filtering, so it is a setting a device may refuse.
Moments do not max-combine, so S1's static half needs its own answer here.

**S3. PCSS for the sun**, as the point lights already have.

**S4. Volumetric fog without compute.** A half-resolution raymarch through
the cascade atlas and the clusters, started at a blue-noise offset, upsampled
by depth. Grows out of `light_shafts.frag`. Depends on L6, R3.

## 7. Content

**M1. `KHR_materials_ior`, `specular`, `clearcoat`.** F0 changes and a second
GGX lobe.

**M2. `sheen`, `anisotropy`.** Charlie distribution with its albedo-scaling
table; anisotropy uses the tangents the loader already has.

**M3. `transmission`, `volume`,** then `dispersion` and `iridescence`: a
copy of the opaque scene sampled by transmissive draws, its mips giving
roughness. One pass more, which the frame graph can hold.

**M4. `KHR_materials_variants`, `KHR_animation_pointer`** for the editor and
configurators.

The writer learns each extension the loader does, and the four `required`
refusals in `gltf_test.dart` turn into loads.

**C1. Gaussian splats.** Read `KHR_gaussian_splatting` into `SplatCloud`
(opacity is linear there and logit in `.ply`); replace the comparator with a
16-bit-key radix sort; re-sort only when the camera has moved enough, a frame
or two stale; draw after the opaque half, depth-tested without writing.

**C2. Occlusion culling on the engine's own rasteriser.** A Masked Occlusion
Culling style buffer at about 256×128, a few hundred conservative occluder
triangles, BVH nodes and renderables tested against it. No latency,
deterministic, identical on every backend; opt-in per scene.

**C3. Hi-Z from last frame's depth**, for scenes with no good occluders: a
depth downsample packed into RGBA8, read back late, reprojected on the CPU,
with "draw everything" as the answer on a cut.

**C4. Octahedral impostors** as the last level of a `LodGroup`, baked with
ordinary passes and drawn as instanced billboards blending three views.

**C5. Automatic LOD on import.** `simplifyMeshWithAttributes` wired into
`flutter3d_build`, error-driven targets, a permissive mode for foliage, the
chain written as `MSFT_lod` and `MSFT_screencoverage`, which the loader
already reads.

## 8. Agents

**A1. A screenshot and an account of what is on it.** `flutter3d_editor_mcp`
draws through `CpuDevice` as `flutter3d_model_mcp` already does, lifting the
refusal §15 records, and answers from an object-id pass with, per entity,
pixel coverage, screen box, depth range and what hides it.

**A2. Claims backed by replays.** `flutter3d_sim_mcp` queries — where is X
after N steps of this tape, did it touch Y — answered with the replay digest,
so any claim can be re-run bit for bit.

**A3. Checking generated assets.** An import step for GLB from TRELLIS.2 or
Hunyuan3D that checks and repairs units, pivot, triangle and texture budget
and duplicated materials, then renders seven fixed views on the CPU.

**A4. Seeded generators.** Scatter, room and corridor as recipe plus seed in
the level, not their baked output.

## 9. Order, and what "all of it in 0.8" costs

```
H1 ─┬─ R1 ── R2 ── R3 ─┬─ L5
    │                  └─ S4 ─┐
    ├─ L6 ────────────────────┘
    └─ (every new block below)
H2 ── measures everything after it
H3 ── H4
H5 ─┬─ L4 (after L3)
    └─ H6
independent: L1 L2 L7 S1 S2 S3 M1→M2→M3 M4 C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 A1 A2 A3 A4
```

Thirty-three items in one version is the biggest release this repository has
cut, and the one real risk is that 0.8 stays unpublished for months while the
packages on pub.dev stay at 0.7.2. Two things keep that honest: every item
lands behind a setting that is off by default, so the golden sets move only
when an item's own scene is added; and the version goes out when the contract
items (H1–H6) and the frame items (R1–R3) are in, even if some content and
agent items move to 0.8.x. That is a suggestion, not a decision already
taken.

Each new golden scene moves the test counts `tool/structure.dart` holds
README, ARCHITECTURE.md and the site to.

## 10. The rest of the research

Placed in [`engine_0_8_plan.md`](engine_0_8_plan.md) on the owner's decision
to include everything (2026-09-23):

- **Phase 7, built in 0.8:** reactive mask for TAAU (R4), EASU spatial
  upscale (R5), motion blur (R6), local exposure (R7), weighted blended OIT
  (R8), HDR output (R9), energy-preserving diffuse (L8), transient aliasing
  and memoryless attachments (H7), retiring the hand-kept binding flags (H8),
  SPZ (C6), splat LOD and streaming (C7), per-texture transforms (C8),
  chunked clusters for scans (C9).
- **Phase 8, on an upstream trigger:** push constants (H9), indirect draw and
  GPU culling (H10), GPU splat sort (H11), subgroups and bindless (H12),
  Slang (H13), neural texture compression on load (C10).
- **Phase 9, lab only:** screen-space radiance cascades (X1), differentiable
  shading on the CPU rasteriser (X2, whose core moves into N1), a neural
  lighting model (X3), frame extrapolation (X4).
- **Phase 10, from the 2026 research (2026-09-24):** a light optimizer after
  LightOpt (N1), adaptive quality from a measured table with motion-aware
  resolution (N2), even frame pacing with a CI spike gate (N3), k-DOP
  clipping in TAAU (N4), splats without sorting (N5), six-way lit particles
  (N6), assets per device class after Roblox SLIM (N7).
- Whether `KHR_gaussian_splatting` is ratified: the research passes disagreed
  (release candidate in February 2026; one found it listed as ratified).
  Check before C1 claims it.
