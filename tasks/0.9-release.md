## Decided, second round (Dmitrii, 2026-10-08)

- Presets: I find the data in open sources (NIST/NBS reports, FPL's Wood
  Handbook, IAFSS proceedings, open papers by Babrauskas and Tewarson —
  never pirated copies of the SFPE or Ignition handbooks), put the useful
  PDFs into Cortex, and then add the materials from what they give.
- The platformer's runner pushes no harder than its grip, μ(mg − buoyancy):
  shallows slow it; tapes and goldens re-recorded. *Done* with μ = 0.68
  (Pollard et al. 2015, dry boot soles) and the game's gravity of 24 m/s².
  Shin-deep water settles a walk at 5.11 m/s. This changes dry running
  too: a walk takes 0.37 s to reach, not 0.09 s, and a sprint 0.61 s.
  Top speeds, braking, air control, dash and slide are as they were, and
  `grip: 0` gives back the old push exactly.
  - Open: the core's character controller strips its −1 m/s stick-down
    off the speed along a ramp. That costs about 14 m/s² on a 14° ramp.
    The runner adds back what it measures it lost (`_rampLoss`, saved in
    rewinds); the real fix belongs in `flutter3d_physics`.
  - Open: the package's own `Water` swim volumes set no `lift`; only the
    demo's wading does.
- Splash drop size: find Mundo, Sommerfeld and Tropea (1995) or another
  measured size law for drops on a film; as is until then.
- Mist share: from a measured share of a fall's water that goes to mist,
  as the package's default.
- Order: my items first (River Sortie's sand and 8 ms, racing's sky, smoke,
  the recordings, the runner's grip, splashes, mist, presets), then the
  full CI and the commits.
- Platforms: push after the commits and fix Windows from CI's logs;
  Dmitrii connects the A55; Impeller on Linux now, once the shadow agent
  has let go of the shaders.
- Recordings: the dungeon's and the strategy's element worlds move into
  their genre packages, so the headless tools build them; the strategy's
  save carries the core's snapshot and its demo version goes up.

## Asked for on 2026-10-08, in 0.9

- **Every step can be switched off, in any combination.** The frame's
  post-processing (bloom, SSAO, fog, SMAA, grade, lens effects, auto
  exposure, …), the passes before the main one (shadows, depth prepass,
  reflections and render textures, caustics, decals), and the elements'
  own steps (flames, smoke, embers, drops, mist, bubbles, the falling
  sheet, firelight, sound). One switch each, independent of the others,
  each combination drawing what is left correctly; a test per step that
  the frame is right without it, and a test over combinations.
- **Callbacks from the effects and the renderer to the game.**
  - The elements' events typed: caught, out, burnt out, wetted, splashed,
    exploded, burner out — callbacks rather than a list to read.
  - What the elements do to the game's bodies: the heat flux on a body
    (ISO 13571's dose with it), the depth and flow of water under it —
    the laws each game now works out for itself, in the package once.
  - The frame: drawn (its `FrameResult`), a pass skipped and why, a
    particle budget run out, a frame over its time.
  - Visibility: a fire or smoke came into sight, smoke stands between the
    eye and a point — for a game's AI and play.
  - Determinism: callbacks that change the run fire from the fixed step,
    in a fixed order, so replays hold; drawing-only ones say so.

- **A fifth genre, exploring an open world, goes in 0.10**, not 0.9:
  `0.10-explore-genre.md`.

## Open questions for Dmitrii (answered above)

- Presets: with the sources found, only TNT and the existing wood (which is
  plywood 1.27 cm's LIFT row) are complete. Oak, pine boards, cardboard,
  thatch, charcoal, the candle (no wax ignition temperature), torch, match
  and blowtorch each lack at least one field. Add them with the engine's
  cited defaults for the missing fields, or wait for the paywalled sources
  (SFPE Handbook, Babrauskas' Ignition Handbook)?
- Mundo's splash-drop size, `8.72·e^(−0.0281K')`, is used far past where it
  was measured (K' ≈ 1000 for 6 mm drops at 9 m/s gives drops of nothing);
  the range Mundo measured is not in any source reached.
- The platformer's runner: the controller's 70 m/s² outpulls the water's
  drag, so only water above the knee slows a sprint; shallows slowing it
  needs the controller's push capped by friction μ(mg − buoyancy), a change
  in `flutter3d_game_platformer`.
- `MistSettings(share: 0.01)` in every app is picked; the water agent
  suggests 0.001.
