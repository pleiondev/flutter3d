# Water, fire and bodies in the games — plan (2026-10-07)

What Dmitrii asked for: the core's water, fire and rigid bodies, drawn as
in `apps/flutter3d_demo_water`, put into the games where they fit, and two
new games. Decided in a round of questions on 2026-10-07.

## Decisions

- **Shared code is a new package**, `packages/flutter3d_effects`: the core's
  water drawn (surface material, falling sheet, spray, bubbles, foam), the
  core's fires drawn (flames, smoke, embers, light, charring), caustics and
  underwater light, sound driven by the physics. Games depend on it; it has
  tests and showcase pages of its own.
- **Order:** the package first, then the two new games as its proving
  ground, then the seven existing games.
- **Recordings** (racing's ghost lap, strategy's match replay) get a new
  version: old ones are refused by version, new ones carry water and fire;
  the replay tests are re-recorded.
- **Platforms:** everywhere each game already runs — desktop, web, Android,
  iOS — with quality presets (fewer particles and water cells on a phone).
- **Sound from the physics:** fire crackle by a fire's watts, a fall's roar
  by its flow, splashes by drops landing, through the engine's audio scene.
- **Style:** both new games realistic, physically lit.

## New games

1. **Stone age** (own characters and names, no borrowed ones — the
   repository is public): an open valley town with tasks — haul stone from
   the quarry, put out a fire, fetch something from the lagoon floor.
   Physics: a car on stone wheels (core vehicle), a long-necked dinosaur
   crane (multibody chain), a volcano whose bombs set huts alight, a river
   with a waterfall into a lagoon and log rafts on the current.
2. **Underwater treasure diver:** reef, a sunken ship, air supply, currents,
   finds lifted with a float. Light: caustics on the floor computed from the
   real surface, light shafts in the water, Snell's window from below,
   absorption by depth (Beer–Lambert, red first).

## Existing games

- **Racing:** fords and puddles (core water: wake, spray, drag on wheels),
  burning wrecks after a hard crash, tyre smoke and dust.
- **River Sortie:** a real river (material, flow, wakes, splashes), burning
  targets, waterfalls at rapids.
- **Dungeon:** live torches that can light wood, barrels and crates that
  burn and spread fire, flooded halls (wading slows), breakable props.
- **Platformer:** water to swim in, fire hazards, physical platforms
  (swinging, sinking, rafts), waterfalls and spray.
- **Strategy:** buildings and forest burn and spread by the wind, rivers and
  fords slow units, siege stones as rigid bodies breaking walls.
- **Sandbox (voxel):** water flowing over blocks, fire through wooden
  blocks, unsupported blocks falling as bodies.
- **Meteor Yard:** meteors leave burning craters that spread to crates,
  debris as rigid bodies.

## The core, asked for on the way (2026-10-07)

- [x] A thick body's surface runs ahead of its mean (heat balance
      integral): a log catches in seconds, not after half an hour.
- [x] `read_fires` gives each flame's reach and axis.
- [x] Shallow liquids are any liquid (`NativeShallowLiquid`,
      `NativeLiquidProperties`): Reynolds-number drag, added mass in the
      integrator, the drag pushed back into the flow, displaced liquid
      keeping its flow, laminar bed friction, molecular viscosity,
      viscous bubbles and spray.
- [x] Momentum-conserving advection in the shallow solver (Stelling and
      Duinmeijer), and the surface slope's push on a body (Froude–Krylov).
- [x] A multibody's motors and limits solved together, motors as servos:
      the crane's neck holds.
- [x] A wheel's damper limited to the mass it carries (a burnt-down or
      empty car no longer bounces off), and tyres that settle the step's
      end velocity (a braked car holds on a slope).
- [x] Contact ignition: where bodies touch, the surfaces meet at the
      effusivity-weighted temperature, and a fuel catches from that spot.
