# Plugins, the loop and events (0.9)

Decided with Dmitrii on 2026-10-08. The whole of it ships in 0.9.0. The aim
is in `vision.md`: anything we can do, the community can do the same way,
by adding a dependency.

## Where we start from

Mapped across the repository on 2026-10-08.

- **Loop.**
  - `GameLoop` and `FixedStep` (1/60 s, at most 5 steps a frame) and
    `StepSystems` phases exist, but only the shooter announces phases.
  - The platformer, the dungeon and racing each write the same frame order
    by hand. Strategy has its own `afterStep` list, and the Flame bridge a
    third order (`HasFixedStep`).
  - Hollow, Reef, Water and the sandbox step a variable `dt`, with no
    determinism and no replays. There is no time scale.
- **Events.** About ten mechanisms:
  - `GameEvents`: one drainer, 256 slots, oldest dropped.
  - `NativeEvent`: a closed set of kinds.
  - `ElementsListener` and `RenderListener`: a single field each.
  - `CollisionListener`, `Signal`.
  - `postGameEvent`, VM extensions, net messages: stringly typed.

  A rollback re-steps and fires sounds and particles again.
- **Extension points.**
  - These exist: `PassContributor`, `RenderNode` (overlay and present
    only), `FullscreenEffect`, `ModelDecoder`, `MaterialDecoder`,
    `StepSystems`, `EntityKind`, `BehaviorKinds`, `ToolTableServer`,
    `GraphicsDevice` and its registry, `PhysicsBackend`.
  - Each registers differently, six ways in all.
  - These are closed: `RenderStep`, `NativeEventKind`, `NativeJointType`,
    `ElementsSteps`, `EditorCommand`, the BT composites, the editor's
    inspector and the build's steps.
  - MCP servers fix their tool lists in the constructor.

## Decisions

### Kernel and plugins

1. **A minimal kernel; everything else is a plugin.** The kernel holds:
   - the scene graph;
   - the frame graph;
   - the device interface;
   - the loop;
   - the event bus;
   - snapshots;
   - the plugin host.

   Effects, elements, genres, formats, backends, tools, photo mode,
   replays and autosave, the Flame bridge, stereo and the laboratories
   are plugins written against the public API. A `standard` preset
   installs today's behaviour in one line.
2. **One plugin unit: `Flutter3dPlugin`.** It has a manifest:
   - name;
   - plugin API version;
   - dependencies;
   - backends;
   - whether it touches the simulation or only the view;
   - permissions.

   `install(host)` registers phases, systems, render steps, decoders,
   events, entity kinds, editor pieces and MCP tools. Registries belong
   to the engine instance; nothing is global.
3. **Discovery.** A package marks itself `flutter3d: plugin:` in its
   pubspec. The build hook generates `plugins.g.dart`, from which the
   engine finds and installs plugins. A list passed in code overrides it.
4. **Plugins are switched at run time.** Dart plugins are compiled in,
   then enabled, disabled and reordered while running, including from
   the editor. Three more kinds load at run time:
   - **data plugins:** `.f3dmat` materials and steps, effect
     descriptions, loop phases and genre rules;
   - **Wasm modules:** sandboxed and deterministic, written in any
     language;
   - **interpreted Dart:** level scripts and mods.

   Shaders compiled at run time only work on WebGL, WebGPU and wgpu.
5. **Versions.** `flutter3d_plugin_api` is a thin package of interfaces
   with its own semver and rare breaking releases. `install` checks the
   version and says plainly what does not match. The Wasm ABI is
   versioned on its own.
6. **Trust.**
   - Dart plugins are trusted as any dependency is.
   - The manifest declares permissions (network, files, simulation),
     and the engine enforces them wherever it hands out access: Wasm,
     data, scripts.
   - Signed packages are used for runs a server verifies and for
     teaching.

### Loop

7. **The engine owns the frame.** `EngineLoop` runs input, the fixed
   steps by phase, the post-step, animation, audio, the camera, the
   render and the UI. Every demo and the Flame bridge move onto it.
8. **Phases.**
   - The engine's step phases are `input`, `movers`, `physics`,
     `elements`, `rules` and `publish`.
   - Its frame phases are `animate`, `audio`, `camera`, `render` and
     `ui`.
   - Genres and plugins add their own phases between these.
9. **Order within a phase.** Order is declared by named `before`/`after`
   constraints and sorted topologically when the loop is built. A cycle
   is an error that names the systems in it. Ties keep registration
   order.
10. **Time.**
    - **Time scale** changes how many steps a second run; `dt` itself is
      untouched. It is recorded in the replay.
    - **The step rate** is a property of the world, as gravity is.
    - **Lost time** is caught up, or announced as an event, instead of
      silently dropped past 5 steps.
11. **Determinism everywhere.**
    - Every demo steps a fixed `dt`, and the arcade's game logic and
      Flame's collisions move into the step.
    - A lint package, `flutter3d_lints`, carries the no-clock,
      `GameRandom` and `Portable` rules to any third-party simulation
      code.
    - In debug the engine steps twice from a snapshot, compares hashes
      and names a plugin that diverges.
    - The render is deterministic too: particles, animation and sound
      are seeded by the step, not the frame.
    - The structure scans cover `apps/`.
    - World constants come from the world and substance constants from
      their presets; only physical constants are literals.

### Events

12. **One typed bus with two channels.**
    - The step channel is collected in the fixed step and handed to
      every subscriber in a fixed order.
    - The frame channel serves sound, particles and UI.
    - The core, the elements, collisions and the renderer publish into
      it. `ElementsListener` and `RenderListener` become subscriptions.
13. **Replays and rollback.**
    - A `resimulated` flag marks steps run again on a rollback or a
      scrub. The frame channel does not hand them out again; it
      reconciles them with what was already shown.
    - A digest of the events is written into `.f3drun`, so a divergence
      is found to the event and tools read events without playing.
    - The events tools see have a schema generated from the types, and
      MCP and the editor subscribe through it.
    - `NativeEventKind` is opened, so a plugin or a new element adds its
      own kinds.

### Renderer

14. **Anchors on every engine pass, and an open `RenderStep`.**
    - A plugin's step goes after shadows, before transparency, before
      tone mapping, and so on.
    - It becomes a full `RenderStep`, with its switch, its needs and its
      report in `FrameResult`.
    - The engine's own post steps are installed the same way.
15. **Shaders.**
    - `.f3dmat` is the recommended language and is built for every
      backend, CPU included. It gains full-screen and compute shaders.
    - Raw per-backend shaders are allowed. A missing backend switches
      the step off and reports why.

### Opened sets

16. **Elements.**
    - `ElementsSteps` is opened.
    - Joints and forces are opened through a solver hook.
    - A new element is a Dart hook over the world: it reads and writes
      the world's fields (heat, water, wind, forces) in its own step
      phase, with `Portable` math and its own view and snapshot. Wasm
      takes the heavy laws.
    - Fire and water move onto the same hook.
17. **Editor, AI and build.**
    - The editor's commands, inspector sections, components and palette
      are opened.
    - So are the behaviour-tree composites.
    - The `buildAssets` steps (importers, bakers) and the `AssetSource`
      resolver are opened.

### Genres, editor, MCP

18. **The four genres become plugins** on the public API, and genres can
    be combined. The fifth is a plugin from the start.
19. **A plugin brings everything.** That means components, inspectors,
    palette entries and MCP tools. The tools merge into one project MCP
    server, namespaced by plugin.

### Community

20. Four things are built for the community:
    - **A template:** `flutter3d create plugin --kind
      render-step|effect|genre|element|tool`, with tests, and examples
      in a repository of their own.
    - **Conformance and a badge.** The suite checks determinism, every
      backend, the switch and the budget.
    - **A catalogue on the site**, read from pub.dev by tag, with the
      API version, the backends and a run in the browser.
    - **MCP tools for authors.** An agent creates, tests and runs a
      plugin through conformance, and the agent skills ship in the
      package.

## Order of work

Each step leaves the tree green. Replays and goldens move only where a
step says so.

1. `flutter3d_plugin_api`, the plugin host, the manifest and
   `plugins.g.dart` discovery. *Done, together with step 2, but its tests
   have not run yet.*
   - The plugin API package has no dependencies; discovery lives in
     `flutter3d_build` (`dart run flutter3d_build:plugins`, plus the hook).
   - A view plugin is refused anything in the step.
   - Still open from steps 1–2: the Level `stepRate` key and the
     `standard` preset. `ElementsListener`/`RenderListener` publish onto
     the bus since step 5.
2. `EngineLoop` with phases, constraints, time scale, the step rate from
   the world and catch-up. Then the event bus with its two channels,
   `resimulated` and the digest in `.f3drun`.
3. Renderer anchors and the open `RenderStep`. Then the engine's post
   steps are moved onto it, with goldens unchanged. *Done, but its tests
   have not run yet.*
   - `RenderAnchor` (fifty, in `flutter3d_plugin_api`) and `RendererSteps`
     (`Renderer.renderSteps`, handed to the host as a registry).
   - Every engine pass goes through the anchored schedule; the registration
     order was compared against a capture taken before the change, 244
     frames, byte for byte, and is held by `render_anchors_test.dart`.
   - Still open: a missing backend switching a plugin step off (decision
     15), and output-sized plugin nodes are decided by anchor only.
   - The engine's post steps have providers now: `RendererSteps.provide`
     and the six families of `1.0-addons.md`, with their own preset,
     `flutter3d_addons_standard`. The passes themselves are still the
     kernel's; that file lists why, pass by pass.
4. The demos onto `EngineLoop`. The variable-step ones get a fixed step
   and replays, and the arcade and Flame logic move into the step. *Done,
   but its tests have not run yet.*
   - Every demo and the Flame bridge run on `EngineLoop`, and `GameLoop`
     is deprecated (removed in 2.0.0). ARCHITECTURE.md §9.1 has each
     game's step order.
   - The dungeon, racing and strategy keep their exact step order, so
     their tapes replay as they were. The platformer's do not: its
     elements step in the `elements` phase now, after the genre step, in
     place of the `ElementalDynamics` wrapper, and its snapshot keeps them
     under `parts`. Its tapes and the Ascent goldens are re-recorded.
   - Hollow, Reef, Water and the sandbox step a fixed 1/60 s. None of them
     records a run yet: the sandbox needs only a recorder and a snapshot;
     the other three need their controls moved onto `InputState` first.
   - `HasFixedStep` builds an `EngineLoop` (`createEngineLoop`), its
     `fixedUpdate` and stepped components are systems, and
     `HasFixedStepCollisions` runs Flame's collision detection in the
     step. River opts in; the arcade's rules moved into `fixedUpdate`, and
     it has no Flame hitboxes to move.
   - Racing's net room is asked through `relayRoom()` with the racing
     simulation's version, and strategy writes a pose record.
   - Still open: the dungeon, racing and the platformer build their run
     files by hand rather than through `DemoRecording.attach`, so the
     loop's journal and the event digests are not in them yet.
5. The four genres as plugins, and fire and water onto the element hook.
   *Done, but its tests have not run yet.*
   - Each genre exposes a `Flutter3dPlugin` (ARCHITECTURE.md §12.1) and the
     demos install it. `EntityKinds` in `flutter3d_sim` fills the entity
     kind slot. `combined_genres_test.dart` installs three genres in one
     loop.
   - `HeadlessGame`: the shooter, the platformer and racing have one.
     Strategy has none: it needs an order verb in the simulation's MCP
     server and a level reader inside the package. Handing a plugin's
     headless game to that server is MCP work.
   - `ElementHook`, `ElementPlugin`, `ElementSwitches` and
     `ElementEventKinds` in `flutter3d_effects`; fire and water are hooks
     inside `Elements`; `NativeEventKind.plugin` opens the Dart side of the
     kinds. `ElementsListener` and `RenderListener` publish onto the bus
     through `toBus`.
   - Still open: a listener's view-only events (`budget`, `seen`, `lost`)
     land on the step channel when `Elements.update` runs inside a step.
6. Editor, MCP, build and BT registries. *Done, but its tests have not run
   yet.*
   - Each slot is filled by its own package, as `RendererSteps` fills the
     render one: `ModelDecoders`/`Decoders`, `EntityKinds`, `EditorPieces`,
     `McpTools`; BT kinds through `BehaviorKindsRegistry`, with open
     composites (`BehaviorComposite`).
   - Editor: `DocumentCommand` sealed over `EditorCommand` and
     `PluginCommand`; components keyed by kind (`builtInComponents` go the
     same way); palette entries.
   - MCP: tools as `<plugin id>.<tool>`, merged into every
     `ToolTableServer` through `projectTools`, and `ProjectMcpServer`. Plugin
     tools are snapshotted by the plugin's package.
   - Build: `BuildStep` around the built-in `models`, `materials` and
     `plugins`, through `buildAssetsWith`; `AssetSources` resolves a
     `scheme:` path.
   - Joints and forces: `NativeForceField` over `NativeWorld`, in the
     physics step, with snapshot-able state; `NativeJointType` stays closed.
   - Still open: the editor app and the servers started from `bin/` do not
     create the registries yet, and `loadModelAsset` takes no `decoders:`.
7. Data plugins, then Wasm modules, then interpreted Dart. *Done, but its
   tests have not run yet.* All three are in `flutter3d_plugin_runtime`.
   - **Data plugins.** `.f3dplugin` (JSON, `f3dplugin` 1, v1 fixture) holds
     phases, events, entity kinds (`DataEntityKind`), materials, render
     steps, Wasm systems, scripts and effects. `effects` names `.f3dfx`
     documents (`flutter3d_particles`' `EffectDocument`, item 4 of
     `tasks/1.0-scope-additions.md`) by `source` or in place, installed into
     `ParticleEffects` with their bus triggers; any other entry is kept and
     not loaded, as version 1 kept the section. `DataPluginLoader` enforces
     permissions: a declared and ungranted permission refuses the plugin; a
     URL needs `network` and `fetch`; a path out of the plugin's directory
     needs `files` and `readFile`.
   - **Shaders at run time.** `RuntimeShaders` builds a material for CPU
     and WebGL2, and a raw GLSL ES render step for WebGL2. Impeller is
     refused for good. On WebGPU a material without a `light` block is
     spliced as WGSL into the engine's compiled `Unlit` stage
     (`spliceMaterialWgsl`, host from `webGpuMaterialHost()`), with no
     compiler in the browser; a `light` block, the `instance` input and maps
     other than the base colour are refused there with the reason, and a
     raw render step stays WebGL2's. A render step in the material language
     waits for its full-screen stages (decision 15).
   - **Wasm: decided, an interpreter.** ABI 1 (`wasmPluginAbi`) is i32 only,
     with four imports and no I/O. A pure Dart decoder, validator and
     interpreter of about 1,500 lines runs on every platform, metered by
     fuel; it is the runtime replays use. The browser's WebAssembly is
     offered on the web, unmetered.
   - **Interpreted Dart: decided, an interface only.** `dart_eval` 0.8.5
     (BSD-3-Clause, maintained) needs `analyzer ^8`, and the workspace's one
     lock file holds analyzer 13 for `tool/api`. It would also ship a
     compiler to every player. `ScriptRuntime` is the seam; no runtime
     ships, and an engine without one refuses a script by name.
8. `flutter3d_lints`, the debug double-step check, scans over `apps/`,
   conformance, the template, the catalogue, and tools for authors. *Done,
   but its tests have not run yet.*
   - `flutter3d_lints`: an analysis server plugin (`analysis_server_plugin`
     0.3.18, the release on analyzer 13.3.0) with four rules. A test holds
     its function list to the structure scan's.
   - `EngineLoop(determinismCheck:)` with `StepDivergence`. It runs only
     with assertions on.
   - Rules 7 and 34 scan `apps/`. The tools and the showcase are excused
     (`notARepeatedApp`), and files that only draw are exempt with reasons.
     Found inside a step, still on the machine's `dart:math`, and now moved
     onto `Portable`, their exemptions removed:
     - racing: `staging.dart` (the starting heading; its runs are
       recorded), `elements.dart` (the ford's shores, drawn beside the
       race), `roadside.dart` (the props' yaw, drawn, with no bodies);
     - arcade: `meteors.dart`;
     - hollow: `props.dart`, `car.dart`, and `terrain.dart` and `crane.dart`
       at load;
     - reef: `main.dart` and `finds.dart`, and `terrain.dart` and `wreck.dart`
       at load;
     - water: `valley.dart` at load.

     Only `staging.dart` reaches a recorded run, so only
     `site/assets/samples/racing.f3drun` moves (`0.9-release.md`). A
     `pow(x, 2)` became `x * x`, and distances stay on `math.sqrt`, which
     IEEE 754 pins. Not scanned and still the machine's: vector_math's
     `Quaternion.axisAngle` and `setRotationYawPitchRoll`, which these files
     call with Portable angles (the hollow car's `rightUp` among them).
   - Conformance: `package:flutter3d_conformance/plugins.dart` has five
     checks and `earnsBadge`. `flutter3d_sim` moved to the second
     publishing tier and the suite to the third.
   - Template: `dart run flutter3d_build:create plugin --kind …`.
   - MCP: `dart run flutter3d_build:plugin_mcp` (`plugin.create`,
     `plugin.conformance`, `plugin.report`), with the skill
     `flutter3d-build-authoring-a-plugin`.
   - Catalogue: `/reference/plugins/`, from `site/content/reference/plugins.json`
     (`npm run plugins`, `--offline`).
   - Still open:
     - the examples repository of decision 20;
     - running the browser on the site;
     - `flutter3d_lints` enabled on this repository itself;
     - the suites of the five demos above run after the move (analysis and
       the structure scan only so far), and `racing.f3drun` re-recorded.
