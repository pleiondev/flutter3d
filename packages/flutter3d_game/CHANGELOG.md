## 0.1.0

* A fixed timestep with an accumulator that reports the steps it had to drop.
* Device-independent input: actions, bindings, a keyboard, a pad and a touch
  layer that all arrive as the same thing.
* Levels as documents — brushes, entities, lights, materials — with a validator
  that refuses one the game could not finish.
* Actors, a flow field, snapshots, replays and a save format.
