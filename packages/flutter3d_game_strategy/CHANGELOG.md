## 0.5.1

* **A fourth genre, and the first crowd.** Units, orders and a step that walks
  them over a `Heightfield` by descending shared flow fields, shoves overlapping
  neighbours apart and sits everybody back on the ground.
* **The shape came out of two measurements rather than out of a preference.**
  Ten thousand agents step in under a millisecond and fifty thousand instanced
  units hold a 120 Hz display, so the package is built for a crowd and not for a
  protagonist: separation is applied to everybody rather than to what a camera
  can see, and the navigation grid is baked at two metres rather than the
  quarter of a metre a shooter needs, which is what makes an order cost half a
  millisecond instead of half a frame.
