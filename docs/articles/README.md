# Source articles for the physics presets

These are the documents that the fire, splash and mist presets of `flutter3d_physics_native` take their
numbers from. Only documents that may be redistributed are kept here:

- **US Government work**, written by employees of NIST/NBS, USDA Forest Service / Forest Products Laboratory,
  FAA, DOE or NASA as part of their duties. These are in the public domain in the United States
  (17 U.S.C. §105).
- **Open-access articles under a Creative Commons licence** (CC BY 3.0/4.0). Attribution is the citation in the table.

Documents that are free to read but have no redistribution licence (IAFSS proceedings, theses, journal
preprints, standards bodies' documents) are not stored. They are listed at the end with their URLs.

The PDF files themselves are not tracked in git (`docs/articles/*.pdf` is ignored): 46 of them weigh
191 MB, which is more than a checkout should carry for numbers that the tables below already cite.
Fetch any of them by the URL in its row; the file name is the one the table gives.
