# Content Policy and Copyright Notices

Version 1.0, effective 16 September 2026.

## 1. Where this applies, and where it does not

The Modeller is a tool that runs on your machine. **We host nothing you make
with it.** Models you open, edit and export never reach us, and there is no
gallery, marketplace, cloud folder or sharing feature in which we could store
or publish your work. There is nothing for us to moderate and nothing for us to
take down, because nothing arrives.

This policy therefore applies to the places where content *does* reach us or
the public through something we operate:

- the source repository at <https://github.com/pleiondev/flutter3d>: issues,
  discussions, pull requests and anything attached to them;
- the website at <https://flutter3d.pleion.dev>, including the gallery, the
  demos and the sample assets shipped with them.

## 2. What you are responsible for

You are responsible for having the rights to the files you open, import,
convert and export with the Modeller. A format converter is not a licence: a
model you are not allowed to use does not become one you are allowed to use by
passing through a GLB export.

The same holds for anything you post to our repository or send us: post only
what you have the right to post.

## 3. What is not welcome in our spaces

In issues, discussions and pull requests: content that infringes somebody's
rights; material that is unlawful where we operate; sexual content involving
minors, which will be reported to the authorities rather than merely deleted;
harassment, threats and targeted abuse; malware, and code whose purpose is to
attack somebody; spam.

We remove such content and may block the account that posted it. The
repository's `CODE_OF_CONDUCT` governs conduct more broadly.

## 4. Reporting content you believe infringes your rights

If you own rights in material that appears in our repository or on our website,
write to **legal@pleion.dev** with the subject line "Copyright notice". This is
our designated address for such notices, under the US Digital Millennium
Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512) and under the European Union's Directive
2000/31/EC and Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (the Digital Services Act) alike.

Include:

1. Your name, address, telephone number and email address.
2. Identification of the work you claim is infringed: a URL, a registration,
   or enough detail to identify it.
3. Identification of the material you say infringes it, with a URL or a path
   specific enough for us to find it.
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised
   by the rights owner, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and, under
   penalty of perjury, that you are the rights owner or authorised to act on
   their behalf.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

We aim to acknowledge a complete notice within five working days and to act on
a well-founded one promptly. We will tell whoever posted the material what was
removed and why, and pass your notice on to them unless the law says otherwise.

**Counter-notice.** If your material was removed and you believe that was a
mistake or a misidentification, write to the same address with the subject line
"Counter-notice", identifying the material and where it was, stating under
penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake,
and giving your name, address, telephone number and consent to the jurisdiction
of the courts where you live. We will pass the counter-notice on to the
complainant and may restore the material where the law allows.

**Abuse of this process.** A notice sent in bad faith, to silence criticism
or over material you do not own, carries consequences under § 512(f) DMCA and
under Article 23 of the Digital Services Act, and we treat repeat bad-faith
notices as such.

## 5. Third-party assets we ship

The repository and the demos include sample models from the **Khronos glTF
Sample Models** set, under their own licences and attributions, together with
the attribution files that came with them. They are there so that a performance
measurement runs on the same mesh everywhere. They are not ours, and their own
terms govern what you may do with them.

If you believe an asset we ship is misattributed or wrongly licensed, tell us
at the same address above. We want those reports.

## 6. Contributions

Contributions to the repository are accepted under the MIT Licence of the
project. By opening a pull request you confirm that you wrote what you are
contributing or have the right to contribute it, and that you license it under
those terms. We do not require a separate contributor licence agreement.

Do not paste code into a contribution from a source whose licence you have not
checked. This includes output from an AI coding assistant where the assistant
reproduced somebody's code verbatim.
