# Where Cobble Hollow's pictures and models come from

Everything here is CC0 (public domain dedication): free to use, change and
redistribute, with no attribution required. The authors are named anyway.

Textures were shrunk to 512 or 1024 px; models had their own textures shrunk
and their transparent materials switched to alpha-tested ones. Nothing else
was changed.
